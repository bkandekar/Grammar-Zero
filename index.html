⚡ Exam Error Spotting Moment SSC CGL Tier-II & Bank PO
✕
Incorrect: "Neither the teacher nor the students was present in the seminar."
✓
Correct: "Neither the teacher nor the students were present in the seminar."
The Proximity Rule in Correlative Conjunctions

When two subjects are joined by 'Neither... nor' or 'Either... or', the verb must agree in number with the closer subject. Here, 'students' (plural) is closer to the verb, requiring were.

Daily Grammar Challenge

Can you spot the error in this real competitive exam question?

Spot the Error Question of the Day
"One of the candidates who has submitted their credentials was found to have falsified their test scores."

Rule Breakdown:

Relative Pronoun Antecedent Rule: When 'one of the + plural noun' is followed by a relative pronoun (who, which, that), the verb in the relative clause agrees with the plural antecedent ('candidates'). Therefore, it must be who have submitted!

Notice that the main verb 'was found' correctly matches 'One'. This is one of the top 5 exam traps in SSC CGL and IBPS PO.
